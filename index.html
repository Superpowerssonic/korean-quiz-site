Why this version works

This structure is simple enough for first-time site owners but polished enough to use in public from day one.

No backend

Everything runs in the browser, so you can host it cheaply and update it by replacing one file.

Auto-switch ready

You can replace or rotate quiz sets by editing one data block, so content updates are much easier for non-developers.

English-first UI

English learners understand what to do immediately, while the quiz content stays focused on Korean.

YouTube-ready

Each lesson can become one dedicated quiz page linked directly from your video description.
